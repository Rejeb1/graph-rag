Pierre Curie was a French physicist born in Paris in 1859. Before meeting Marie Curie, he worked at the School of Industrial Physics and Chemistry (ESPCI) in Paris, where he and his brother Jacques Curie discovered the phenomenon of piezoelectricity in crystals. Pierre Curie became a professor at the University of Paris. He died in a street accident in Paris in 1906, five years before Marie Curie won her second Nobel Prize.
